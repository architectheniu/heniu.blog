Rocket Lab (RKLB) revenue increased in the third quarter by 72.7% year over year to 267.83 million USD, while earnings per share were −0.12 USD compared to −0.03 USD a year earlier.

### Company guidance

The company expects revenue in the fourth quarter of 2026 in the range of 270.00 million USD – 290.00 million USD. The company forecasts gross margin (GAAP) at 34.0% – 36.0%, and adjusted gross margin at 39.0% – 41.0% in the fourth quarter of 2026. The company forecasts operating expenses (GAAP) in the fourth quarter of 2026 at 162.00 million USD – 168.00 million USD, while adjusted operating expenses are at 136.00 million USD – 142.00 million USD. The company expects net interest income of 45.00 million USD in the fourth quarter of 2026. The company forecasts adjusted EBITDA in the fourth quarter of 2026 at −20.00 million USD – −14.00 million USD.

### What management says

Management emphasized that there is a lack of capacity in the rocket launch market, which was anticipated by the company. According to management, after the fabrication of a new Neutron rocket tank dome, the issue with the faulty component has been resolved, although the tank is awaiting testing. Management emphasized that an exact debut date for the Neutron rocket will not be provided until the tank passes all tests. According to management, with rising demand, higher prices for Electron and HASTE launches should be expected. Management emphasized that the company could have signed more Neutron launch contracts, but decided on higher prices with fewer launches. According to management, negative cash flows will not permanently reverse before the Neutron rocket enters production without the acquired Iridium, and the picture is expected to change after the closing of the acquisition.
