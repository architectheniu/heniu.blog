Teradyne (TER) revenue in Q3 was 1.31 billion USD (+69.8% y/y), and earnings per share (GAAP) increased to 2.17 USD (+189.3% y/y).

### Quarterly results

Earnings per share (GAAP, basic) stood at 2.19 USD (+192.0% y/y), while earnings per share (adjusted) was 2.29 USD (+169.4% y/y).

### Company guidance

In Q4 2026, the company expects revenue in the range of 1.25 billion USD – 1.35 billion USD, with adjusted earnings per share at 1.95–2.25 USD.

### Management commentary

Management expects significant growth in the memory segment in the coming year, which is expected to encompass all segments, with a particular focus on HBM technology. According to management, inventories are increasing due to preparations for growth and extended lead times for component deliveries for testers; therefore, the company is aggressively increasing production capacity in the area of memory testers and power test lines. Management emphasized that growth is expected to accelerate quarter by quarter in the coming year, although the company avoids specific forecasts due to the volatility of customer test line assembly cycles. According to management, the robotics portfolio is expanding, and solid growth is expected from the largest customer in the coming year. Management remains interested in co-packaged optics technology, but its development pace depends on profitability allowing for increased production.
