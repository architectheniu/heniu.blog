Analog Devices (ADI) revenue increased in the quarter by 42.2% year over year to 4.37 billion USD, and earnings per share increased by 112.5% year over year to 3.40 USD.

### Quarterly results

Gross margin was 69.3% compared to 63.1% a year earlier (+6.2 pp year over year). Operating income reached 1.87 billion USD compared to 945.21 million USD a year earlier (+97.7% year over year), and net income was 1.65 billion USD compared to 787.74 million USD a year earlier (+109.5% year over year). Operating margin was 42.7% compared to 30.7% a year earlier (+12.0 pp year over year), while adjusted earnings per share was 4.06 USD compared to 2.26 USD a year earlier (+79.6% year over year).

### Company guidance

The company expects revenue in the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 to be in the range of 4.25 billion USD to 4.55 billion USD. The company forecasts operating margin (GAAP) in the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 in the range of 38.8% – 41.8%, adjusted operating margin at the level of 49.5% – 51.5%, and earnings per share (GAAP) from 2.82 USD to 3.22 USD. The company also forecasts adjusted earnings per share in the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 in the range of 3.62–4.02 USD.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that demand remains strong, and order cancellations are staying at normal levels, which allows the company to believe it is still in the middle of the cycle. According to management, artificial intelligence is only at the beginning of its expansion as a key technology that will affect all other technologies. Management emphasized that due to a strong project portfolio and growth dynamics, confidence in future growth has increased, resulting in further expansion of production capacity. According to management, production capacities are tight both in their own factories and with external manufacturers, which is a phenomenon observed across the entire industry. Management also emphasized that customers are solving increasingly difficult problems at the intersection of the physical and digital worlds, for which the company is prepared to provide increasing value. According to management, there are no signs yet of significant inventory rebuilding among customers.
