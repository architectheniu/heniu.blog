Old Dominion Freight Line (ODFL) revenue in Q3 was 1.59 billion USD (+13.1% y/y), and earnings per share (reported) increased by 28.9% y/y to 1.65 USD.

### Quarterly results

Earnings per share (reported, basic) reached 1.66 USD (+28.7% y/y) compared to 1.29 USD in the previous year.

### Company guidance

Capital expenditures in FY 2026 are expected to be 380.00 million USD.

### Management commentary

According to management, demand remains stable, and discussions with customers indicate an improving demand environment. Management emphasized that higher shipment weight allows maintaining stable revenue per unit of weight without fuel, confirming the effectiveness of the pricing policy. Management also indicated that most of the increase in cost per employee results from rising supplemental benefit costs related to inflation in healthcare. According to management, the company has a large reserve of capacity in real estate and equipment, and due to in-house driver training, it does not experience issues with employee recruitment. Management noted that by maintaining the current revenue level and seasonally better-than-usual results, the operating ratio should be at the lower limit of the normal range of deterioration.
