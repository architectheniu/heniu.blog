PepsiCo (PEP) revenues in the third quarter were 25.27 billion USD (5.6% y/y), and reported earnings per share increased by 17.4% y/y to a level of 2.23 USD.

### Quarterly results

Adjusted earnings per share stood at 2.34 USD (2.2% y/y). Revenues in the PFNA segment were 6.50 billion USD.

### What management says

Management emphasized that organic growth of 3.1% represents the fastest pace since the fourth quarter of 2023, with growth driven by volumes in beverages globally (+3%) and snacks globally (+4%). According to management, expiring commodity price hedges are causing an increase in their costs, which will be partially visible in the fourth quarter. Management stated that the company is not satisfied with the results in North America and is open to revising the distribution model and the business model. According to management, non-growth related expenses are to be eliminated to redirect funds to brands driving growth. Management expects gradual improvement in the fourth quarter and in the next year.
