Starbucks (SBUX) revenue fell by 4.5% y/y in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 to 9.14 billion USD, while net income increased by 408.8% y/y to 677.20 million USD.

### Quarterly results

Operating income was 991.80 million USD (+256.5% y/y), and earnings per share (GAAP) stood at 0.59 USD, with adjusted earnings per share of 0.80 USD. In the Company-operated coffeehouses segment, revenue fell by 5.5% y/y to 7.43 billion USD, whereas the Licensed coffeehouses segment saw an increase of 9.4% y/y to 1.20 billion USD, while the Other segment shrank by 16.0% y/y to 515.50 million USD.
