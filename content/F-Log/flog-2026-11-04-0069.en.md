Astera Labs (ALAB) revenue increased by 153.7% year over year in Q3 to 584.90 million USD, and earnings per share (reported) increased by 98.0% year over year to 0.99 USD.

### Quarterly results

Earnings per share (reported, basic) was 1.05 USD, representing a 94.4% year over year increase compared to 0.54 USD the previous year.

### Company guidance

In Q4 2026, the company expects revenue in the range of 730.00 million USD – 750.00 million USD and earnings per share (GAAP) at the level of 1.13–1.17 USD. The company also forecasts gross margin at approximately 72.0% and adjusted earnings per share (non-GAAP) in the range of 1.62–1.66 USD.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that the business model is generating increasing leverage, as operating costs are growing slower than revenue. According to management, Scorpio X switches are expected to continue recording growth thanks to new customers and platforms. Management indicated that Orion rack solutions are expected to improve margin through software sales and licensing fees. According to management, the transition to optical connectivity increases the company's chances, as it earns significantly more from it than from copper solutions. Management also noted that the new Orion rack is not dependent on a single accelerator supplier.
