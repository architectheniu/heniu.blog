Vertex Pharmaceuticals (VRTX) filed a report of changes in management or board of directors.

Insiders sold shares for 26.1 million USD, including 26.1 million USD from 10b5-1 plans; market purchases amounted to 25 thousand USD. The largest sale was reported by Micron Technology (MU) insiders: 25.7 million USD.

The President, Chief Technology Officer, and Chief Operating Officer of Micron Technology (MU) sold 28,545 shares valued at 25.7 million USD under a 10b5-1 plan.

The Executive Vice President, Chief Financial Officer, and Chief Operating Officer of QUALCOMM (QCOM) sold 2,500 shares valued at 0.4 million USD under a 10b5-1 plan.
