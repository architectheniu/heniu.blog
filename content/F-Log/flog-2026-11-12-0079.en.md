Nebius (NBIS) revenue grew 571.3% year over year in the quarter to 980.80 million USD, while earnings per share was −0.13 USD compared to −0.50 USD a year earlier.

### Company guidance

The company expects revenue in 2026 to be in the range of 3.00 billion USD to 3.40 billion USD, currently expecting the lower end of this range. The company forecasts revenue in 2027 in the range of 12.00 billion USD – 13.00 billion USD. The company expects capital expenditures in 2026 to be around 27.00 billion USD, which is a value above the previous range of 20–25 billion USD. The company forecasts capital expenditures in 2027 to be in the range of 40.00 billion USD to 45.00 billion USD. The company expects adjusted EBITDA margin in 2026 to be around 40.0%.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that demand for artificial intelligence is real and large, and building capacity and its financing are solvable tasks. According to management, customers are committing for longer, at higher prices and paying more upfront, which has made prepayments a standard element of contracts. Management emphasized that at its own locations, the company is on or even ahead of schedule, although there are delays at some colocation locations, of which customers are being informed. According to management, none of this capacity has been canceled, and the company will be ready for the moment when supply and demand in the industry balance out.
