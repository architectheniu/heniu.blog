NXP Semiconductors (NXPI) revenues in Q3 were 3.79 billion USD (+19.3% y/y), and earnings per share (GAAP) increased to 3.48 USD (+40.3% y/y).

### Quarterly results

Earnings per share (GAAP, basic) reached 3.51 USD (+40.4% y/y), while earnings per share (adjusted) was 4.20 USD (+35.0% y/y).

### What management says

Management emphasized that no widespread inventory replenishment is being observed in the automotive sector, with longer lead times improving demand visibility. According to management, confidence in continued growth is based on the increasing adoption of software-defined vehicles and the impact of artificial intelligence on industry and the Internet of Things. Management indicated that the gross margin improvement is structural and results from higher revenues, better fab utilization, and operational efficiency, while price increases merely cover higher costs. According to management, there is a structural shift in pricing within the industry, allowing semiconductors to capture a larger share of value in systems. Management noted that in data centers, growth depends on product launches, their adoption, and software deployment by customers. According to management, the company already has won projects in North America and the Asia-Pacific region, and is also conducting several pilots in China and Europe.
