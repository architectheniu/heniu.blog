Ross Stores (ROST) revenue increased in the quarter by 11.7% year over year to 6.26 billion USD, and earnings per share increased by 20.9% year over year to 1.91 USD.

### Quarterly results

Operating income was 766.79 million USD compared to 648.50 million USD a year earlier (+18.2% year over year). Net income reached 606.32 million USD compared to 511.94 million USD a year earlier (+18.4% year over year). Operating income on a quarterly basis was 766.79 million USD compared to 1.10 billion USD in Q2 (−30.5% q/q).

### Company guidance

The company expects comparable store sales growth in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 at a level of 4.0% – 5.0%. The company forecasts earnings per share in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 in the range of 2.16–2.25 USD, with earnings per share growth during this period expected to be 8.0% – 13.0%. The company also forecasts earnings per share for the full fiscal year 2026 in the range of 8.75–8.84 USD.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that growth is being driven mainly by increased foot traffic in stores and the addition of new and returning customers. According to management, the company still sees significant opportunities for gaining market share, and many initiatives are in the early stages. Management stated that the company does not feel competitive pricing pressure that could harm its business. According to management, during the holiday season, results may be weighed down by more expensive domestic transportation and higher expenditures on store wages and marketing.
