Exelon (EXC) revenues in the third quarter were 7.32 billion USD.

### Quarterly results

Earnings per share (GAAP) stood at 0.70 USD, while earnings per share (adjusted) was 0.87 USD.

### Company guidance

The company expects adjusted earnings per share (non-GAAP) – “Adjusted (non-GAAP) operating earnings per share” in FY 2026 to be 2.85–2.91 USD.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that investors building large facilities, including data centers, are ready to commit financially and are willing to sign transmission security agreements. According to management, energy storage provides a necessary tool in the fight against the crisis of price affordability and supply reliability. Management noted that they only include what is realistic and sustainable in their plans, with a potential upside. According to management, the company supports maintaining the price cap on capacity auctions until better ways to reduce costs are found.
