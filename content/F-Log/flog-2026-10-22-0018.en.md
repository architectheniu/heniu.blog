Honeywell International (HON) revenues in Q3 were 4.99 billion USD (+1.3% y/y).

### Quarterly results

Operating margin was at 14.8% (6.4 pp y/y).

### Company guidance

The company expects revenues (FY 2026) in the range of 20.00 billion USD – 20.10 billion USD, while in Q4 2026 it forecasts organic revenue growth at a level of 5.0% – 6.0%.

### What management says

Management expressed high confidence regarding further improvement in sales and margin in the industrial automation segment due to the rapid improvement of this business's fundamentals. According to management, project activity in the area of process automation and technology remains very strong and diversified. Management emphasized that acquisitions represent an option for the company, and a key direction for development would be expanding the offering in the field of measurements and sensors. According to management, maintaining price increases requires ensuring good customer service and the continuous introduction of new products. Management indicated that the projected slowdown in organic growth at the end of the year results from caution, while short-cycle orders are very strong. Management also emphasized the implementation of artificial intelligence, stating that all teams have already been trained or are currently undergoing training.
