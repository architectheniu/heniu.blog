Palo Alto Networks (PANW) revenue grew 39.2% year over year to 3.45 billion USD, and earnings per share grew 21.3% year over year to 0.57 USD.

### Quarterly results

Net income was 482.00 million USD compared to 334.00 million USD a year earlier (+44.3% year over year). Adjusted earnings per share was 1.05 USD compared to 0.93 USD a year earlier (+12.9% year over year). Operating income was 96.00 million USD compared to 309.00 million USD a year earlier (−68.9% year over year).

### Company guidance

The company forecasts revenue in the second quarter of fiscal year 2027 at 3.53 billion USD – 3.55 billion USD and for the full fiscal year 2027 in the range of 14.52 billion USD – 14.57 billion USD. The company expects adjusted earnings per share in the second quarter of fiscal year 2027 to be 1.01–1.03 USD, and for the full fiscal year 2027 to reach the level of 4.26–4.30 USD.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that demand was broad, and all of the company's platforms accelerated revenue growth. According to management, higher component costs across the industry will weigh on gross margin throughout the fiscal year. Management emphasized that demand for computing power will grow, however, the company intends to invest prudently and maintain high free cash flow margins even during periods of higher capital expenditures. According to management, companies are still in a very early stage of AI implementation, and most agent applications are still in the testing and pilot phase.
