American Electric Power (AEP) revenues increased in Q3 2026 by 13.7% y/y to 6.83 billion USD, and earnings per share (reported) increased by 19.8% y/y to 2.18 USD.

### Quarterly results

Adjusted earnings per share was 2.23 USD, representing a 23.9% y/y increase compared to 1.80 USD the previous year.

### Company guidance

The company expects earnings per share in FY 2026 in the range of 6.25–6.55 USD and 6.11–6.41 USD, while in FY 2027 it forecasts 6.75–7.05 USD, and in FY 2028 7.40–7.70 USD.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that they see a great deal of potential investment extending beyond the announced plan and that demand is already occurring, which requires the construction of new generation capacity. According to management, signed customer connection agreements in Texas are real, and despite adopting a cautious plan in this region, the company still sees strong growth there. Management also indicated that they view the idea of creating a separate generation company positively and are checking where it will bring the greatest benefits to customers. According to management, share issuances must increase earnings per share, as the company wants to remain cautious in managing shareholder capital.
