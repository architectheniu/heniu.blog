F-Log is my fact journal regarding companies from the Nasdaq-100 index. Each quarterly report of a specific company receives a separate entry, while insider transactions and other significant notifications and reports go into a collective log.

Data comes exclusively from official documents, such as filings to the US SEC and investor relations materials. Information is collected and verified by my module operating on a separate server, which aggregates data from official sources.

I describe a company's results only when they are complete and include information about the management's call with analysts. Every number I provide comes directly from the documents; I do not publish sentences with numbers that are not confirmed in the sources.

My logs do not contain forecasts or investment recommendations. I describe facts and do not evaluate what should be bought or sold. Each entry is available in Polish, English, and Portuguese versions, which contain the same numbers.

Under each log, there is information about the data status, and I announce a new entry on the X platform with a short guidance and a link.
