# T-Mass and Core Density

**In Antigravity cosmology:** Total System Mass (T-Mass) and Core Gravitational Field (Core Density / Heavy Nine).
**In standard terms:** Aggregate market capitalization of the NASDAQ-100 universe and concentration metric in mega-cap issuers (>1T USD).

## What is T-Mass

T-Mass (Total Mass) is the aggregate market capitalization of the 100 constituent issuers of the **NASDAQ-100** index expressed in USD Trillions.

* **NASDAQ-100 Universe (Not just tech):** The index comprises the largest non-financial companies listed on Nasdaq — containing consumer discretionary leaders (Amazon, Tesla), retailers (Costco), and healthcare/biotech alongside pure technology companies.
* **Valuation Delta (Repricing):** The session change in T-Mass (24h Delta) measures **aggregate market valuation repricing**, not net external cash inflow. When share prices rise, market capitalization expands without requiring equivalent dollar amounts of cash to be deposited into the market.

## What is Core Density

The **Core (Jądro)** is the dynamic cohort of NASDAQ-100 companies whose individual market capitalization exceeds **$1 Trillion USD** (historically and narratively referenced as *Heavy Nine*):

* **Core Density (%):** Percentage share of total index T-Mass represented by >$1T companies.
* **Gravitational Dominance (> 50–60%):** Extreme index concentration. When a handful of Core companies accounts for over 60% of total mass, their price movements dominate index returns over the remaining 90+ peripheral constituents.
* **Core Membership Shifts:** A company crossing above the $1T threshold (*singularity emergence*) or dipping below it (*gravitational collapse*).

## Average Share (Peripheral Temperature)

Telemetry also tracks the **Average Share**:
* Calculated as the unweighted (equal-weight) nominal share price average of non-Core peripheral constituents.
* While nominal prices depend on share counts and stock splits, its daily delta serves as a quick proxy to verify whether the broad periphery is participating in index expansion or if market gains are concentrated exclusively in mega-caps.
