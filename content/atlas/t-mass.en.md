# T-Mass and Core Density (Heavy Nine)

**In Antigravity Cosmology:** Total System Mass (T-Mass) and Core Gravitational Density (Heavy Nine / Core Density).
**In Classical Language:** Total combined market capitalization of NASDAQ-100 components and index concentration in megacap tech (> $1T market cap).

## What T-Mass Really Is

T-Mass (Total Mass) is the aggregate market capitalization of all 100 NASDAQ-100 companies, measured in US Dollar Trillions. 

The 24h Session Delta reflects the net capital inflow or outflow across the entire US tech sector.

## What Core Density Measures

Core Density represents the percentage share of total index market capitalization held by entities valued at **over $1 Trillion USD** — the Heavy Nine (e.g. Apple, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Broadcom, Tesla).

* **Density > 50-60%:** Extreme gravitational concentration. A handful of megacaps dictate index velocity, while the remaining 90 components have limited directional impact.
* **Core Anomaly Events:** When a company crosses the $1T threshold (Emergence) or falls below it (Collapse), altering the gravitational balance.
