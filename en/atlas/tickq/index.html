TICKQ — Nasdaq Market Breadth, a.k.a. Background Radiation

2026-08-10
#atlas  #TICKQ  #market breadth  #microstructure 

TICKQ — Nasdaq Market Breadth#

In the Antigravity cosmology: Background Radiation; its SMA200 average is the Mean Radiation. In classical language: a breadth indicator — the number of Nasdaq stocks ticking up at a given instant minus the number ticking down.

What TICKQ really is#

An index price can lie through concentration: five Core singularities can drag NDX upward while hundreds of smaller bodies fall. TICKQ measures what the index price cannot show: whether the whole galaxy is breathing together. A reading of +600 means that at this second six hundred more stocks are ticking up than down — a broad market, a move carried by mass. A reading of −600 is panic across the entire population, regardless of what the giants are doing.

That is why a divergence between price and TICKQ is so telling: an index rally on negative background radiation is a narrow rally — lifted by the Core, with no participation from the periphery. Such moves obey a different physics than those where the background glows together with the Star.

How we measure it on the bridge#

Two readings, both with thresholds hard-coded in the configuration. Instantaneous TICKQ: from −600 (panic) through sell-off and testing zones up to +600 (euphoria). Mean Radiation (SMA200): the session’s backdrop — below −150 we describe a market bottom, above +150 a market top, with zones of breakdown, sell-off, testing and accumulation in between. The instantaneous reading says “what now”; the average says “in what climate, for hours”. The narrative may dress these numbers in cosmos, but it may not move them.

Operating Window (Spot Session Only)#

A crucial rule of market mechanics: the TICKQ indicator is active strictly during the regular cash session in New York (9:30–16:00 ET). During the futures session (overnight trading, pre-market, and off-hours NQ trading), TICKQ is dormant and frozen because the thousands of constituent Nasdaq stocks are not trading in regular continuous auction. Heniu analyses TICKQ background radiation exclusively when the cash equity market is open.

What TICKQ does not do#

It does not predict — extreme readings can mark a turning point or the beginning of an even deeper move. On this blog TICKQ is a gauge of the breadth of the present moment: whether the move is carried by the mass, or only by the Core.

An Atlas card. Zero signals — only the physics of the System.
