### I. Atlas of the Three-Body System: Gravitational Cosmology

The technology market — the US 100 — is not a single point. It is a gravitational system in which three main bodies interact with one another. Understanding their roles is the key to moving beyond "guessing".

1.  **The Sun: NDX Index (Cash Market / SPOT)**
    * **Role:** The base mass and the source of truth. NDX represents the physical shares of the 100 largest companies on the Nasdaq exchange. This is where institutional capital operates, without leverage.
    * **Significance:** Any move that is not confirmed by the mass of NDX is merely speculative fluctuation. NDX is the gravitational core. This is where the market buys ownership rights in companies.

2.  **The Probe: NQ Futures Contracts**
    * **Role:** The fast scout. A leveraged instrument used for Price Discovery.
    * **Significance:** NQ is the first to react to emotions and news. It is extremely fast, but often "hollow" — it can generate a violent price jump with no real cash-market capital behind it. It is animated to a large extent by HFT algorithms.

3.  **The Dyson Sphere: QQQ ETF (Options Structure)**
    * **Role:** The link and the structural barrier. QQQ is where retail meets institutions, and massive options volumes form "gravitational walls". This is where, to a large extent, speculation without leverage plays out.
    * **Significance:** The levels of Implied Volatility (IV), together with the Put/Call Ratio and OI (Open Interest) on QQQ, mark the boundaries from which price bounces or within which it gets trapped. Among other things, this is where the Gamma Walls are found, configuring the daily volatility ranges.

### II. Mechanics of the System: Arbitrage and Divergences

The strength of this methodology lies in observing the desynchronization of these bodies.

* **Arbitrage:** This is the invisible force binding all three instruments together. Algorithms make sure that the prices of NQ, NDX and QQQ remain consistent. When one of them "runs away" too far, arbitrage violently pulls it back. This happens in fractions of a second. It takes an enormous force of capital on an instrument to overcome arbitrage, and even then it is so fleeting that a screenshot showing different candle colors across the instruments is like finding a blue lobster.
* **Divergence:** This is a crack in the pattern. The strongest signal for the Architect. If the Probe (NQ) races upward while the Sun (NDX) stands still or loses mass (negative CVD) — we know the move is false and a collapse will follow. These differences occur between various elements of the system and their indicators, such as price or CVD. This is a systemic anomaly. The Blue Lobster.

### III. The Data Purity Protocol

True navigation requires rejecting the noise. Derivative and indicative charts do not exist in objective reality — they are merely an approximation created by brokers and content providers.

A modern navigation system must rest on the **Infrastructure of Truth**:
1.  **Live Data:** Current market data in real time.
2.  **CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta):** The aggression counter. It makes it possible to distinguish aggressive buying from passive absorption.
3.  **TICKQ (the NASDAQ Galaxy):** The pulse of the entire ecosystem (7,000+ companies). Its moving average (SMA200) shows whether the "galaxy" is expanding (a broad bull market) or contracting (the index being pushed up by a handful of companies).

### IV. Kinetics and Evidence: Examples from the Sessions

The logs on heniu.blog document specific anomalies that confirm this physics:

* **Speculative Profit-Taking:** A situation in which price stands still, NDX CVD is positive (institutions are holding), but NQ CVD is sharply negative. Here we see speculators taking profits while the base (Spot) remains intact.
* **Accumulation in the Void:** The moment when price is falling and panic on NQ reaches its zenith, but NDX sees a one-trillion-dollar increase in capitalization within a single session. This is the "concreting" of the bottom by institutional capital, invisible to traditional analysts.
* **Golden Flow:** A session in which all instruments and their indicators take the same upward, accumulative vector.
* **ADR Util (Fuel):** Our indicator showing the percentage utilization of the average range. When ADR Util reaches 90-100%, we know that the market's kinetics have died out and it is time for sideways movement (Mean Reversion). At the same time, when ADR exceeds 100%, this is strong information about the strength (momentum) of the move in a given session; it is usually a value that correlates with strong market sentiment.
* **MOC (Market on Close):** This is not just information about capital being "parked". It is the critical moment of Book Squaring by the Market Makers. An MOC volume of several billion dollars in one direction often determines the direction of the next session's open (Gaps).
