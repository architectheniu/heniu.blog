Palantir Technologies (PLTR) revenue in the third quarter was 2.31 billion USD (+95.9% year over year), and GAAP earnings per share increased to 0.44 USD (+144.4% year over year).

### Quarterly results

GAAP earnings per share was 0.47 USD (+135.0% year over year), while adjusted earnings per share reached the level of 0.44 USD (+109.5% year over year).

### Company guidance

The company expects revenue in Q4 2026 at the level of 2.65 billion USD and for the full year 2026 at the level of 8.53 billion USD. According to guidance, adjusted operating income (non-GAAP) is expected to be 1.64 billion USD in Q4 2026 and 5.25 billion USD for the full year 2026, with adjusted free cash flows (non-GAAP) in the range of 4.80 billion USD – 5.00 billion USD for the full year 2026.

### What management says

Management emphasized that commercial customers are moving from discussions to the pilot and implementation phase significantly faster, with initial contracts being shorter but undergoing significant expansion. According to management, the company cannot keep up with demand, which forces a shift of human resources toward inbound customers due to limited capacity. Management indicated that current results stem to a greater extent from the rapid growth of the entire market than from the company's own effectiveness. According to management, the value of artificial intelligence is being created at the level of processes within an organization, and not just by increasing the efficiency of individual employees. Management also noted that customers using Sovereign AI workshops are implementing solutions with great haste, which, according to management, is only the beginning of this trend.
