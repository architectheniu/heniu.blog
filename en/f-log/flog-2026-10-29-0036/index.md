Honeywell Aerospace (HONA) revenues increased in the third quarter by 2.5% y/y to 4.64 billion USD, and earnings per share (reported) increased by 12.8% y/y to 2.29 USD.

### Quarterly results

Earnings per share (reported, basic) was 2.29 USD (+12.8% y/y).

### Company guidance

For the full year 2026, the company expects organic growth at a level of 4.0% – 5.0%, adjusted earnings per share in the range of 7.85–8.00 USD, pro forma standalone adjusted EBIT of 4.38 billion USD – 4.42 billion USD, and free cash flows at a level of 1.20 billion USD – 1.40 billion USD.

### What management says

Management emphasized that the gap between the company's needs and deliveries from suppliers is narrowing. According to management, with increasing deliveries, the priority is serving aircraft manufacturers to maintain the continuity of their production lines, before fulfilling orders for the aftermarket. Management indicated that the company is directing a larger number of deliveries toward US national security needs than to foreign customers, while the order backlog shows no declines. According to management, electronic component shortages have not significantly impacted operations, and no changes in customer behavior resulting from oil prices were observed in passenger aviation. Management also stated that negotiations for renewed contracts with manufacturers are proceeding as expected and will allow for a better alignment of prices with costs.
