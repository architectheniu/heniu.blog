NVIDIA (NVDA) revenue grew by 103.2% year over year in the third quarter of fiscal year 2027 to 115.84 billion USD, and earnings per share grew by 107.7% year over year to 2.70 USD.

### Quarterly results

Gross margin was 74.8% compared to 73.4% a year earlier (+1.4 pp year over year). Operating income reached 77.52 billion USD compared to 36.01 billion USD a year earlier (+115.3% year over year), and net income was 65.29 billion USD compared to 31.91 billion USD a year earlier (+104.6% year over year). Adjusted earnings per share was 2.68 USD compared to 1.27 USD a year earlier (+111.0% year over year). Revenue in the Compute & Networking segment was 106.0 billion USD compared to 50.9 billion USD a year earlier (+108% year over year). Revenue in the Graphics segment was 9.8 billion USD compared to 6.1 billion USD a year earlier (+62% year over year).

### Company guidance

The company expects revenue in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2027 in the range of 126.10 billion USD – 133.90 billion USD. The company forecasts adjusted gross margin at a level of 71.0% – 72.0% in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2027.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that the company's momentum is real, and demand is accelerating while supply is simultaneously increasing. According to management, the company has secured supplies of memory and advanced chip packaging. Management also emphasized that in the allocation of limited supplies, all customers receive the same prices.
