PACCAR (PCAR) revenue in Q3 was 7.94 billion USD (+19.0% y/y), and net income increased by 43.6% y/y to 847.50 million USD.

### Quarterly results

Earnings per share (GAAP) was 1.61 USD (+43.8% y/y), which is identical to the earnings per share (GAAP, diluted) result of 1.61 USD (+43.8% y/y).

### What management says

Management emphasized closer cooperation with suppliers to ensure parts necessary for truck production. According to management, customers are starting to place orders for next year, which is only the beginning of this process. Management expects further growth in parts sales by the end of the year and gaining market share. According to management, uncertainty should subside, and the market will strengthen over the next year. Management also mentioned that the new PACCAR Connect platform operates like an open system with applications that can generate additional revenue.
