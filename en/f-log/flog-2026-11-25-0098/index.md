Workday (WDAY) revenue grew by 11.2% year over year in the third quarter of fiscal year 2027 to 2.71 billion USD, and EPS grew by 2.1% year over year to 0.96 USD.

### Quarterly results

Operating income was 329.00 million USD compared to 259.00 million USD a year earlier (+27.0% year over year). Operating margin stood at 12.1% compared to 10.7% a year earlier. Net income was 237.00 million USD compared to 252.00 million USD a year earlier (−6.0% year over year). Adjusted EPS was 2.82 USD compared to 2.32 USD a year earlier (+21.6% year over year).

### Company guidance

The company expects subscription revenue in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2027 to be at the level of 2.60 billion USD and for the full fiscal year 2027 to be at the level of 9.958 billion USD. The company forecasts subscription revenue growth in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2027 by 10.0%, and for the full fiscal year 2027 by 13.0%. The company expects adjusted operating margin in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2027 to be at the level of 31.7%, and for the full fiscal year 2027 to be at the level of 31.5%.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that the company delivers AI-based products that customers can use today. According to management, customers are moving from experimenting to implementing and paying for AI solutions. Management noted that demand for AI is strong and monetizing these products is gaining momentum. According to management, in the coming years, helping customers transition from investing in AI to its mass usage with measurable results will be key. Management expressed optimism for the next year due to the wide range of new products. The CEO views the AI consumption-based billing model as an opportunity for margin growth in the future.
