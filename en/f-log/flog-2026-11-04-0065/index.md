Booking (BKNG) revenue in the third quarter was 8.89 billion USD (−1.3% y/y), while earnings per share (GAAP) increased by 6.8% y/y to 3.61 USD.

### Quarterly results

Earnings per share (adjusted) reached 4.66 USD (+17.1% y/y), while operating income was 3.42 billion USD (−1.9% y/y). The company's net income was 2.72 billion USD (−1.1% y/y).

### What management says

Management emphasized that the traffic generated by AI tools is currently small, but the company is capturing most of this traffic, which, given the current dynamics, is an early stage. According to management, collaboration with AI agent creators aims to build economic advantage by diversifying traffic sources. Management noted that in some regions, especially in Europe, regulations may slow the adoption of personal AI agents. According to management, small and medium-sized hotels can count on the company's support, as fees are only collected after the guest's arrival. Management also stated that preliminary bookings for next year look healthy, giving the company confidence in its outlook.
