CSX (CSX) filed a report on entering into a material definitive agreement, termination of a material definitive agreement, and financial obligation.

Insiders sold shares for 54.1 million USD, including 49.7 million USD from 10b5-1 plans; market purchases amounted to 0. The largest sale was reported by Cisco Systems (CSCO) insiders: 18.2 million USD; second: Booking (BKNG) insiders: 8.1 million USD.

CSX: 8-K: 1.01 material definitive agreement, 1.02 termination of agreement, 2.03 financial obligation (Document: 8-K 1.01,1.02,2.03,9.01; Filing date (US time): 17.11; 8-K Content 1.01 (entering into a material definitive agreement): agreement: Material Definitive Agreement; quote (EN): „On November 17, 2026, CSX Corporation (“CSX”) entered into a new $1.2 billion five-year senior unsecured revolving credit agreement (the “Credit Agreement”) by and among CSX, as borrower, the lenders party thereto (the “Lenders”), and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., as administrative agent.”; 8-K Content 1.02 (termination of a material definitive agreement): agreement: Material Definitive Agreement; quote (EN): „Effective November 17, 2026, CSX terminated its $1.2 billion senior unsecured revolving credit agreement, dated as of February 28, 2023, among CSX as borrower, the lenders party thereto, and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., as administrative agent (as amended, the “Prior Credit Agreement”).”; 8-K Content 2.03 (new financial obligation): quote (EN): „The information set forth in.”; Source (SEC): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/277948/000095010326017684/0000950103-26-017684-index.htm).
