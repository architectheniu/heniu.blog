Vertex Pharmaceuticals (VRTX) revenue in Q3 increased by 11.8% year over year to 3.44 billion USD, while earnings per share (GAAP, basic) fell by 39.1% year over year to 2.58 USD.

### Company guidance

For the full year 2026, the company expects revenue in the range of 13.15 billion USD – 13.25 billion USD, including "PALSONIFY" sales revenue at a level of 40.00 million USD – 50.00 million USD.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that contacts with the US regulator regarding povetacept are proceeding as planned and the company is awaiting a decision date. According to management, povetacept has been manufactured by the same manufacturer since the beginning of the studies, which allows for a calm assessment of the application and production issues. Management also pointed to interest from nephrologists in povetacept and the desire to use it once it is approved for marketing. According to management, acting on the cause of kidney disease ensures better effects, which increases confidence in the results of anaxaplin. Management noted that in the case of the pain medication JOURNAVX, it is consciously maintaining a patient support program to acclimate physicians to new habits until full reimbursement is ensured. Regarding cystic fibrosis, management stated that the selection of the best next-generation drug will only occur after the results of all candidates are obtained.
