Keurig Dr Pepper (KDP) revenue increased in Q3 by 70.5% year over year to 7.34 billion USD, and earnings per share (GAAP, diluted) increased by 22.4% year over year to 0.60 USD.

### Quarterly results

Operating income was 1.33 billion USD (+34.0% year over year) with net income at 952.00 million USD (+43.8% year over year).

### Company guidance

For 2026, the company expects revenue in the range of 25.90 billion USD – 26.40 billion USD.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that price reductions and promotions in the single-serve coffee segment are appropriate steps for the business, even though they weigh on some of the profit in the short term. According to management, weaker demand for pods and a larger share of private labels are transitional and cyclical phenomena that may last longer. Management noted that work on the company split is proceeding according to schedule. According to management, the company is optimistic about the energy drink market and highly values its position in this sector.
