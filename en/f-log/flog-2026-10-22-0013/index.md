Microsoft (MSFT) filed a proxy statement.

Insiders sold shares for 6.3 million USD, including 6.3 million USD from 10b5-1 plans; market purchases amounted to 50 thousand USD. The largest sale was reported by CrowdStrike (CRWD) insiders: 4.6 million USD.

George Kurtz, President and CEO of CrowdStrike (CRWD), sold 20,000 shares valued at 4.6 million USD under a 10b5-1 plan.

Javier Olivan, COO of Meta Platforms (META), sold 1,575 shares valued at 1.1 million USD under a 10b5-1 plan.

2 other transactions were also reported.
