Cisco Systems (CSCO) revenue increased by 24.4% in the quarter to 18.52 billion USD, and earnings per share increased by 58.3% to 1.14 USD.

### Quarterly results

Operating income was 5.60 billion USD compared to 3.36 billion USD a year earlier (+66.5% y/y). Net income reached 4.55 billion USD compared to 2.86 billion USD a year earlier (+59.2% y/y). Adjusted earnings per share was 1.40 USD compared to 1.00 USD a year earlier (+40.0% y/y).

### Company guidance

The company forecasts revenue in the second quarter of fiscal year 2027 at 18.00 billion USD – 18.20 billion USD, and for the full fiscal year 2027 at 74.80 billion USD – 75.80 billion USD. The company expects adjusted gross margin in the second quarter of fiscal year 2027 at 65.0% – 66.0%. The company forecasts adjusted operating margin in the second quarter of fiscal year 2027 at 36.0% – 37.0%. The company expects adjusted earnings per share in the second quarter of fiscal year 2027 in the range of 1.34–1.36 USD, and for the full fiscal year 2027 in the range of 5.47 USD – 5.53 USD. The company forecasts earnings per share (GAAP) in the second quarter of fiscal year 2027 in the range of 1.07–1.09 USD, and for the full fiscal year 2027 in the range of 4.38–4.44 USD.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that the company is at the beginning of a large networking investment cycle driven by AI deployments. According to management, earnings are growing faster than revenue, indicating strong operating leverage. Management emphasized that hyperscalers are already deploying network architectures that connect multiple data centers for AI workloads. According to management, enterprise customers are investing in their own data centers as well as in running AI applications in the cloud. Management emphasized that the company does business with neocloud-type companies, while remaining cautious regarding their credit risk. According to management, component supply is stable, and the company expects to achieve its raised annual guidance.
