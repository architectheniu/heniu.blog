Tesla (TSLA) filed a proxy statement for the annual meeting.

Insiders on 19.10 sold shares for 24.3 million USD, including 23.7 million USD from 10b5-1 plans; market purchases were 0. The largest sale was reported by Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) insiders: 9.3 million USD. The largest sale was reported by CrowdStrike (CRWD) insiders: 7.0 million USD.

The Executive Vice President and General Manager of DSG at Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) sold 17 262 shares valued at 9.3 million USD under a 10b5-1 plan.

The President and CEO of CrowdStrike (CRWD) sold 20 000 shares valued at 4.7 million USD under a 10b5-1 plan.

16 other transactions were also reported.
