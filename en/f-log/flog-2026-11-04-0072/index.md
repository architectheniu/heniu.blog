Shopify (SHOP) revenue in Q3 was 3.74 billion USD (+31.6% y/y), and earnings per share (GAAP) increased by 80.0% y/y to 0.36 USD.

### Quarterly results

The company's operating income reached 568.00 million USD (+65.6% y/y), while net income was 462.00 million USD (+75.0% y/y).

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that personal AI agents represent an entirely new sales channel that allows merchants to acquire new customers. According to management, purchases made by AI agents are in the early stages, but their growth rate is clearly strong. Management noted that when a customer reaches a store via an AI agent, the company's earnings remain the same as with other methods of reaching a customer. According to management, despite the shift in marketing and infrastructure spending over time, nothing has fundamentally changed in the company's costs and profitability. Management also indicated that the purchase of the shop.com domain is a declaration of more ambitious plans for the Shop app as a shopping destination. According to management, brick-and-mortar sales do not constitute a separate business, but are based on the same platform, catalog, and buyer relationships.
