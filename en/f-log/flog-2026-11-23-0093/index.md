CrowdStrike (CRWD) revenue increased in the quarter by 28.3% year over year to 1.58 billion USD, and earnings per share was 0.02 USD compared to −0.03 USD a year earlier.

### Quarterly results

Adjusted earnings per share was 0.34 USD compared to 0.24 USD a year earlier (+41.7% year over year). Net income reached 24.86 million USD compared to −26.78 million USD a year earlier.

### Company guidance

The company forecasts revenue in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2027 at a level of 1,638.10 million USD – 1,644.10 million USD. The company expects adjusted earnings per share in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2027 at a level of 0.36 USD.

### What management says

Management emphasized that the better-than-planned results are a result of sales pressure, gross margin improvement, and greater operational efficiency. According to management, security has become a driving force, and the company is becoming an entry point for deploying AI in enterprises. Management believes that the timing for the acquisition is right and points to long-term preparations for technology integration. According to management, the combination with the new entity will allow it to become the largest company in the field of AI security.
