Walmart (WMT) revenue grew in the third quarter of fiscal year 2027 by 5.2% year over year to 188.84 billion USD, while earnings per share fell by 19.5% year over year to 0.62 USD.

### Quarterly results

Operating income was 7.77 billion USD compared to 6.70 billion USD a year earlier (+16.0% year over year). Adjusted earnings per share reached 0.65 USD compared to 0.62 USD a year earlier (+4.8% year over year).

### Company guidance

The company expects net sales growth at constant exchange rates in fiscal year 2027 to be in the range of 4.9% – 5.2%. The company forecasts adjusted operating income growth at constant exchange rates in fiscal year 2027 in the range of 8.0% – 8.6%. The company expects adjusted earnings per share in fiscal year 2027 to be in the range of 2.84–2.89 USD. The company forecasts that capital expenditures as a percentage of net sales in fiscal year 2027 will be approximately 4.0%.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that e-commerce was the main driver for the quarter and that the company continues to gain market share. According to management, AI agents are starting to play an increasing role in shopping, and the company is well-prepared for new ways of buying. Management emphasized that results for the combined membership package are encouraging and the company's goal is to build the most essential membership in the United States. According to management, the growth of e-commerce is driving the growth of profitable services, such as advertising, the seller platform, or membership. Management emphasized that the customer remains stable and resilient, and operating income will grow faster than sales next year.
