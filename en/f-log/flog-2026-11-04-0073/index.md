Datadog (DDOG) revenue in the third quarter was 1.20 billion USD (+35.2% y/y), and earnings per share (adjusted) reached 0.81 USD (+47.3% y/y).

### Quarterly results

Earnings per share (GAAP) was 0.32 USD (+220.0% y/y), while earnings per share (GAAP, basic) was 0.33 USD (+230.0% y/y).

### Company guidance

The company expects revenue in Q4 2026 to be in the range of 1.19 billion USD – 1.20 billion USD, and for the full year 2026 in the range of 4.515 billion USD – 4.525 billion USD. In Q4 2026, adjusted operating income (non-GAAP) is expected to be 280.00 million USD – 290.00 million USD, and adjusted earnings per share (non-GAAP) in the range of 0.69–0.71 USD, with full-year 2026 guidance of 2.73–2.75 USD per share.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that earlier contract renewals by large customers are a positive phenomenon, although they constrain revenue in the short term. According to management, customers outside the AI industry are also implementing AI-based solutions, which translates into higher cloud consumption and promises a long period of growth. Management indicated that the group of customers from the AI industry will expand, which will provide more stable results in the future. According to management, earlier contract renewals result from the rapid growth of these customers' companies, rather than from cost-cutting. Management also noted that the Bits AI assistant is the best-received product debut in the company's history. According to management, customers renewing contracts earlier commit to higher spending in exchange for volume discounts.
