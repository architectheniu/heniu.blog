Apple (AAPL) revenue in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 was 114.48 billion USD (+11.7% year over year), and earnings per share (accounting) increased by 13.5% year over year to 2.10 USD.

### Quarterly results

Earnings per share (accounting, basic) was 2.11 USD (+14.1% year over year), with operating income at 35.63 billion USD (+9.9% year over year).

### What management says

Management emphasized that Siri AI serves as an assistant whose capabilities will be gradually expanded while maintaining an emphasis on privacy and security. According to management, the iPhone is set to become the user's intelligent personal hub, as it stores the most important data to which even the company does not have access. Management is optimistic about long-term services growth, pointing to the growing number of active devices as the foundation of this process. According to management, rising memory prices are putting pressure on product prices, making programs that facilitate customer purchases important. Management also noted that in subsequent quarters, the benefit from cheaper inventory will decrease in the face of unprecedented memory prices. According to management, production of the new iPhone Duo is growing, however, it is currently difficult to predict when supply will meet demand.
