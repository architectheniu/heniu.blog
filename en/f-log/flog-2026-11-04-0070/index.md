Amgen (AMGN) revenue in Q3 2026 was 10.37 billion USD (+8.5% year over year), and earnings per share (reported) increased by 19.4% year over year to 7.08 USD.

### Quarterly results

Earnings per share (reported, basic) reached 7.14 USD (+19.4% year over year).

### Company guidance

For 2026, the company expects revenue in the range of 38.90 billion USD – 39.70 billion USD and earnings per share (GAAP) at the level of 18.34–19.28 USD. The company also forecasts adjusted earnings per share (non-GAAP) in the range of 22.90–23.70 USD and capital expenditures at approximately 2.60 billion USD.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that the company intends to maintain expenditure discipline so that shareholders also benefit from acquisitions. According to management, following the update of guidelines, physicians will quickly change treatment methods in favor of the drug IMDELLTRA, which works strongly in combination with chemotherapy. Management expressed expectation of similar efficacy in combination with antibody-drug conjugates and noted that the company is confident in its position as a reliable supplier of biosimilars, designing new production capacities for larger quantities of protein. According to management, daxdilimab for discoid lupus erythematosus will be prescribed mainly by dermatologists, an area in which the company possesses experience and appropriate teams.
