Costco Wholesale (COST) filed a report on changes to its board of directors, indicating the departure of Helena B. Foulkes from her position as a member of the board of directors effective 7.11.2026.

Insiders sold shares for 39.8 million USD, including 37.4 million USD from 10b5-1 plans; market purchases were 0. The largest sale was reported by Airbnb (ABNB) insiders: 21.9 million USD, and the second largest is Vertex Pharmaceuticals (VRTX): 6.3 million USD.
