Autodesk (ADSK) revenue grew 16.6% year over year to 2.16 billion USD, and earnings per share grew 67.5% year over year to 2.68 USD.

### Quarterly results

Operating income was 551.00 million USD compared to 470.00 million USD a year earlier (+17.2% year over year). Net income reached 563.00 million USD compared to 343.00 million USD a year earlier (+64.1% year over year). Adjusted earnings per share was 3.20 USD compared to 2.67 USD a year earlier (+19.9% year over year).

### Company guidance

The company expects revenue in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2027 to be at a level of 2.22 billion USD – 2.235 billion USD, and for the full fiscal year 2027 in the range of 8.36 billion USD – 8.375 billion USD. The company forecasts adjusted earnings per share in fiscal year 2027 at a level of 12.70–12.76 USD and billings in the range of 8.60 billion USD – 8.65 billion USD. The company expects earnings per share (GAAP) in the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2027 in the range of 2.64 USD – 3.02 USD, while for the full fiscal year 2027 in the range of 9.98 USD – 10.35 USD. The company forecasts operating margin (GAAP) in fiscal year 2027 at approximately 27.0% and adjusted operating margin in the range of 39.2% – 39.3%. The company expects free cash flows in fiscal year 2027 in the range of 2.725 billion USD – 2.75 billion USD.

### Management commentary

Management emphasized that the company had a strong quarter and signed the largest corporate contract in its history with a client. According to management, artificial intelligence represents an exceptional opportunity to increase the value of software for customers, and falling AI costs allow for the automation of more processes. Management emphasized that with highly automated processes, the company will earn from some of them. According to management, the acquired MaintainX business slightly exceeded expectations, and interest in maintenance solutions is very high. Management emphasized that contract renewals remain strong, demand in key markets is healthy, and the company enters the final quarter of the year from a position of strength. According to management, long-term growth potential remains relevant, and the company is well on track with its established plans.
