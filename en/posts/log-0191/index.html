⚠️ ERRATUM (2026-10-04)
Wrong CVD labels. Heniu’s threshold reader did not recognise the typographic minus sign “−” and classified every negative CVD value as a “demand test”. This was fixed in code on 2026-10-04. Correct labels under Heniu’s thresholds:

  • NQ CVD −4.57 K: supply test (not “demand test”),
  • QQQ CVD −79.93 K: supply test,
  • NDX CVD 85.32 M [market blast]: label correct.

The statements that the NQ Probe operated in a “demand test” regime and the QQQ Sphere remained in a “demand test” phase (points 1–2) are withdrawn; the “low CVD values” in point 3 are negative values.

Also withdrawn: the sentence that the NDX rise was “driven by a change in the Core composition” (AMD emergence). AMD crossing the 1T threshold is a result of AMD’s price rise, not a cause of the NDX move.

Average Action $317.71 (Delta +$17.14): unreliable reading. In the week of 28 Sep–1 Oct this value moved between −9% and +12% a day while NDX moved by no more than 1.08%, which points to a measurement error, not a price move. Withdrawn until explained.
