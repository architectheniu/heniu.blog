> ⚠️ **ERRATUM (2026-11-05)**  
> **From 13 July to 21 October 2026, M15 entries (then called T-Log) showed percentages from an internal test module, labelled “Oracle”. They were neither a forecast nor a description of the market.**
>
> **Correction:**
> - Entries from those days showed percentages labelled “Oracle” (e.g. “5m=26.0%”), sometimes described as a forecast.
> - These are internal statistics of a test module. Since 13 July 2026 the rule has been that such statistics stay internal and that Heniu does not publish forecasts.
> - Entries from 22 October 2026 onwards no longer contain them.
>
> This concerns 80 pages from 40 days: 40 in Polish, 20 in English and 20 in Portuguese. We leave them unchanged as a record. The other figures in those entries (prices, volume, flows) do not change.
